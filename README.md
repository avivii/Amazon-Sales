## 💡 Motivation & Background
As a Full-Stack developer, I bring extensive hands-on experience in 
software engineering, database architecture, and advanced SQL. 
Driven by a deep interest in data analysis and business strategy—which 
also led me to pursue academic studies in Economics and Management—I 
created this project to expand my capabilities into Business Intelligence.

While writing complex SQL queries is a natural part of my day-to-day 
engineering background, the core learning objective of this project was 
mastering Tableau: transforming raw transaction data into clean, intuitive, 
and executive-level visualizations.

I have a strong drive for continuous learning across the data landscape, 
and I aim to keep growing at the intersection of software engineering, 
analytics, and business decision-making.
